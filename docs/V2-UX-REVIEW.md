# Steward v2 independent UX review

Review verdict: keep one extractor journey; cut speculative screens. Concept boards are not implementation specifications.

1. Select extractor/resource, compare proposal, inspect stock/people, inspect automatic capture readiness entirely by keyboard.
2. 360px and 200% zoom: no page-level horizontal overflow; map/table alternatives available; paired summaries persist.
3. Source capture time, ingestion/retrieval time and game playtime separately labeled. Last saved evidence stays visible after live failure.
4. Known evidence leads; unknown flow terms should be compact, not a wall of dashes. No pointer-only placement or hover-only details.
5. Proposal is an app plan, never relocation/assignment in game. No generic best-worker/location score.
6. Distinct loading/no snapshot/empty/filter-empty/unsupported/service failure/stale/missing baseline messages and useful next actions.
7. Capabilities load automatically. Unsupported delivery/labor counters mean measurement unavailable and stock comparison fallback, not a dead-end fake capture state.
8. No manual measured data entry. A successful capture cannot be shown until service records support it; interruptions preserve partial state without rates.
9. Full balance includes consumption/losses; inventory-only delta remains stock change.
10. Independent per-screen upstream before/after comparison; raster pictures do not count as keyboard/responsiveness QA.

Data gates: #39 identity, #40 comparison contracts, #41 units, #44 capabilities, #54 automatic capture. Research discovered provisional candidate/live identity disagreement; do not attach candidates by actor suffix or proximity alone.
